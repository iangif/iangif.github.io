Hello! I'm a third-year Computer Science student minoring in Linguistics at McGill University.
I'm currently researching neural grammar induction models under Professor [Eva Portelance](https://evaportelance.github.io/) and working as a research assistant at the [Montréal Computational and Quantitative Linguistics Lab](https://mcqll.org/). I'm also a research lead at the [McGill AI Lab](https://www.mcgillailab.com/).
My research interests lie in the realm of cognitively-inspired AI, interpretability, and optimization.

Outside of research, I'm a part of the [Computer Science Undergraduate Society](https://mcgillcsus.com/) and [Linguistics Undergraduate Association](https://www.instagram.com/linguamcgill/), where I organize events and plan fundraisers. In my free time, I enjoy reading and improving my ice skating skills!
